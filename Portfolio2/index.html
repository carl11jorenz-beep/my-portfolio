About Me

I'm a simple person with a slightly complicated personality.
I can hear my motorcycle breaking down louder than I can hear my mom giving orders, and I somehow always end up wearing black, so I guess I'm slowly turning into one too.

I'm also a drummer, or as I like to call it, a professional “tugtugpak” enthusiast.

I usually prefer being alone, but I don't actually like feeling alone. Sometimes I'm a normal human being, and most of the time, I'm just a kamote.

I'm not afraid of my mom, but when it comes to my dad... I suddenly remember how to behave.

I'm not afraid of dying, but losing money? Now that is terrifying.

I also have my own little world where I spend most of my time, probably overthinking things, listening to music, or just being myself.
